# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Working Hours and Flexibility

Northwind Robotics operates on a core-hours model. All employees are expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local time, Monday through Thursday. Outside core hours, you are free to arrange your schedule however suits you, provided you meet your team commitments. Fridays are meeting-free by default: recurring meetings may not be scheduled on Fridays without VP approval.

Full-time employees work a standard 40-hour week. Manufacturing and field service roles follow shift schedules published by Operations at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Engineering, product, and business roles are hybrid: a minimum of two days per week on site at the Lincoln campus, coordinated within each team so that teams overlap on at least one common day. Roles that require lab or production floor access (hardware test, manufacturing engineering, field service) are on site four or more days per week.

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from your VP and a review every 12 months. International remote work is limited to 30 days per calendar year and must be cleared with People Ops in advance for tax and export control reasons, since much of our robotics stack is export controlled.

## Paid Time Off

Northwind offers 25 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly, plus 10 company holidays. Up to 5 unused vacation days roll over into the next calendar year and must be used by March 31. Sick leave is separate and uncapped for absences under five consecutive days; longer absences move to short-term disability handled by People Ops.

We strongly encourage everyone to take at least one unbroken two-week break each year. Managers are responsible for making that possible.

## Equipment and Security

Every employee receives a company laptop with disk encryption and endpoint management enforced. Personal devices may not access production systems, customer data, or robot telemetry. Badge access is required for all lab spaces; tailgating into the robotics lab is a serious safety violation because autonomous units may be under test.

Report lost equipment or suspected account compromise to security@northwindrobotics.example within 24 hours.

## Code of Conduct Basics

We build machines that move around people, so we hold a high bar: be direct, be respectful, and never bypass a safety interlock to hit a deadline. Concerns can be raised to any manager, People Ops, or anonymously through the ethics hotline listed on the intranet.
