# Northwind Robotics Expense Policy

## Principles

Spend company money like it is your own, in the company's interest, with receipts. Expenses are submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of being incurred. Anything submitted later than 90 days is not reimbursable without CFO approval.

Itemized receipts are required for every expense of 25 USD or more. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.

## Travel

Book through the corporate travel tool whenever possible. Guidelines:

- Flights: economy class for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires VP approval in advance.
- Lodging: up to 220 USD per night in standard cities, 320 USD in high-cost cities (the finance portal keeps the current list). Book refundable rates when plans are uncertain.
- Ground transport: use the most economical reasonable option. Rideshare is fine. Rental cars require a business justification when visiting customer warehouse sites, which is usually easy since sites are rarely near transit.
- Booking window: book flights at least 14 days in advance except for customer-impacting incidents or field service emergencies.

## Meals

- Individual meals while traveling: up to 75 USD per day, itemized receipts required.
- Team meals: up to 40 USD per person, the most senior attendee pays and submits.
- Client meals and dinners: up to 120 USD per person with prior manager approval. List every attendee and their company in the expense line. Alcohol is reimbursable at client meals only, capped at two drinks per person.

## Equipment and Software

Peripherals under 150 USD (keyboards, mice, cables, headsets) can be expensed without approval, once per category per year. Anything above 150 USD, and all software subscriptions, go through IT procurement instead of expenses so licensing and security review happen properly.

## Field Service Exceptions

Field service engineers responding to a Sev 1 site outage may book same-day travel without pre-approval and exceed lodging caps by up to 50 percent when no compliant option exists near the customer site. Flag these expenses as "incident response" in the portal and link the incident ticket.

## What Is Never Reimbursable

Commuting to your home office, traffic fines, hotel minibar, in-flight Wi-Fi for personal use, and expenses for spouses or partners unless explicitly approved by a VP for a company event.
